Skills

I consider myself a creative, organized, and resilient person. Throughout my career, I have been able to adapt to challenging projects and meet tight deadlines, always ensuring quality results. I also have strong leadership and teamwork skills, supporting my colleagues with kindness, empathy, and understanding.

I am a communicative person, able to express ideas clearly and assertively, while also listening and empathizing with others’ perspectives. I maintain constant critical thinking that drives me to improve, both personally and professionally, always seeking to learn and contribute more in every experience.

Thanks to my communication skills, I had the opportunity to serve as public image for CID Gallup in social media videos, as well as at different events outside the workplace where I have stood out as a speaker. These experiences have strengthened my ability to communicate messages effectively and build trust.

Watch video ->

My programming skills cover several languages, allowing me to adapt to different contexts and needs. I specialize in Python, which I mainly use for data analysis and creating programs focused on process automation, optimizing time and ensuring efficiency.

In web development, I use the triad of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, complemented with PHP, which enables me to design and program functional web applications. This knowledge is complemented by my experience with SQL and NoSQL databases, considering scalability and security.

I have also worked with Java for basic application development and with C++, especially for physics programming in virtual reality environments.

In the area of analysis, I have strong command of various tools that allow me to handle projects efficiently and accurately. I manage Excel at an advanced level, applying it to large-scale data management, creating visualizations, developing macros, and carrying out complex analyses.

I use SPSS to handle large datasets and generate customized statistical tables. With Power BI, I create interactive and attractive visualizations that make data interpretation easier and support strategic decision-making. I also use PowerPoint as a creative tool to design dynamic and visually engaging presentations.

Additionally, with Python, I perform advanced statistical processes such as imputations, weighting calculations, data cleaning, variable recoding, among others.
